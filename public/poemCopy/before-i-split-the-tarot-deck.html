Let me tell you my fortune. Not with star
dance or first breath; no unbundled sticks
in piles for this game. A toss of coins
is as unuseful as a mess of offal cast
over slate. My root resists such grifts.

No, my fortune speaks in the whirl of tints
that spoke the depth of my eye. Can you see
how the brown soaks to grey, orange flecks
spackled across my wheel? Stare here, stare
hard and deep and read my story of you.

This is my fortune: I see you. My story
is the history I build of us, the marks
I etch in iris to keep you tethered here
to my flesh. I cannot lose you, Mother,
when I spot your brand deep in the mirror.
