There's moss on my hook.
Green spores mist air as I shift.
These manacles chafe.

Rough bricks build landscapes.
I map possibilities.
Dragons perch on tors.

Is it just an ant?
The sun shifts; my window dims.
I map perspectives.

So simple to write.
Haiku must count more than sound.
Ants are not dragons.

These manacles chafe.
My fingers linger on keys.
My code uncompiles.

Things are not the same.
I am more than my ticket.
I code in haiku.

Watch my program run:
see how my dragons break locks!
Blood clots the sunset.
