(After Τα επικίνδυνα by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

Said Myrtías (a student from Syria
in Alexandria at the time of those co-kings
August Constans and August Constantius,
partly pagan, experimenting Christian):
"Strengthened by inquiry and reflection,
I'll not fear my urges like a coward.
I'll give my body to these pleasures,
these dreamed-of indulgences
these dared, erotic desires,
these blood-led lustful passions with
no fear, because when my will determines –
for I have the will, strengthened
as I am by reflection and inquiry –
in these critical moments I'll find
my spirit, once more, ascetic …"
