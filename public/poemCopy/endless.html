(After February 2026)

This need I feel for summaries? It comes
from snaps of images caught, coughed from screens,
from headscream words which won't stay still, and still
there's more to scroll and more to eat and sniff
– a building bursts its glass again, again,
but one in sunshine, one in rain and now
we all must play: which continent was that?
Keep up! No, stop! My thumb, it locks itself

and here I stand and look for food to eat.
I could prepare a wholesome lunch from scratch.
Ultra processed – that's the tag that kills.
My brother died last week. I think that's true.
I know I need to eat, the clock is clear:
my eating time is here and still I feel

– no thing? Nothing much, at least; at least
I have a screen that comforts me in sharp
disclaimers, hard men shouting, bombs and words
of bombs and fuck this, Paul, for gifting me
your silence all these ended years and me
the coward too afraid to break the break
between us: look at this canker of words!
I fucked us, my fear of you and me and I
don't even know the reason why our glass
struck out and look, my thumb, it twitches, moves:
see? Hard men strut the streets of Minneapolis
and, perhaps, Saint Paul?
