"If we plant them round the rim of the bowl
we'll have a cascade of clean whites and reds –
a pole of surfinas shaved from the wall!"

Your hands are filthy, like the grin you wear.
I push my hands deep inside the compost,
carding the bog's history with fingers.

"This sod is as solid as you!" I like
the way your eyes unfocus as you try
to turn my apish hoots into meaning.

"What's that in your prayer?" I trace your sightline
back to the clod between my flat, hot palms.
Like drool, a pink tube of worm pendulates,

lost in cruel atmospheres. "We can crown it
King Worm," I say. "It can live in a hall
of petunia roots high in the sky!"

– And maybe tonight, after we lick muck
from skin and pain and pull sweat from our flesh
we can offer it gifts crafted from dreams.
