The opposite of radiance is – what? An absence?
A loss? A birth of that black sun that consumes
the ash and bone of dead things and alchemists?

I would stare at the sun that casts its first light
through mists that cover the high Dymchurch tide
but even so early its abundance burns a black sun
into my retina and through to the back of my skull
where the ticks of breath, pulse, fear and urge
surge like a flood's wave against a bone sea wall.
I will not flood. I am learning that the shadow lies
not behind me, but within that radiance that daubs
clouds in illusions of shape and colour and weight.

Gold comes not from lead. It is a work of collapse
in the mindless churn of the sun beyond radiance.
Are you yet consumed my brother, sister, mother?
Are you gilded in starlight, or moved beyond suns?

Twelve hours have passed and still I stand here
on the wall that separates the marsh from the sea.
Ghostlight breaches the horizon, soft across waves.
The rock in the sky paints a silver path to my feet
and I would step on it, ride each painted crest
to the dry, radiant face that hides in moon rhymes.

It is not my time. I cannot learn those secrets
that spray and ignite beyond the last breath.
I would cast the bones of the pyre's flames across
this stretch of the whale road and let you ride
their song. I will sketch these words into sand
when the moon reaches the sky's own capstone
and maybe they will catch up with you in a place
beyond time and you will perhaps understand
that even as I strapped lead boots to my feet
I loved you all.
