(After Τα παράθυρα by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

In this dark apartment, where I pass
the heavy days, I wander up and down, around,
to find the windows. For when open
one window will give me consolation.
But the windows are hard to find, or
I cannot place them. And maybe better I don't.
Perhaps the light will bring a tyranny.
Who knows what new things it would show.
