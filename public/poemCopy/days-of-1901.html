(After Μέρες του 1901 by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

This was what was notable about him:
that in all his dissolute grace
and his wide learning in love,
despite all his familiar
harmonies of stance and age,
there were instants – of course
very rare – when an outline escaped
of flesh near intact.

The beauty of his twenty-nine years,
so tempered by pleasure,
offered up an unbalanced reminder:
an adolescent – ungainly in parts – in love
for the first time gives up his pure form.
