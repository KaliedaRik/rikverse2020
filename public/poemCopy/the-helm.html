(After the Sutton Hoo helmet)

I dare not stare – those dark sockets demand
that eyes must sit within your cold copper plates.

Was your last Bretwalda not worthy? Boars snuffle
a skull long leached by the clays of your beacon hill.

Your captain's ship? A rot of wood. The regal jewels
and bones your dragons kept? Stolen rusts and voids.

Ghosts brought the widow to your grave. Spades sliced
daylight onto your crushed weave as new war brewed.

In our nation's cool, sacred space you watch me, demand
flesh enter shape. My eyes are not yours to command!
