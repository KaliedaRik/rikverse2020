This is a sharp book:
it cuts my finger
as I turn the page.

If I push my thumb
hard on my finger:
an egg of blood.

I last read this book
in my parents' bed:
I was their bolster.

My love of letters
hatched in that bed:
mole and rat; toad.

As I turn the page
I stain the paper
with my blood story.

I was meant to glue
your pages together:
I became your bolster.

In my blood entangles
strife, not love:
I ache to hear the piper
who plays at the gates
of a misty dawn.
