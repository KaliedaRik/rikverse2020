If I may, I'll not shake your hand.
We can greet with smiles, inquiries
over health: we both know the lies
such questions impose but we're here
to bury your son. The grief you hold
is beyond my composure so I will not
judge; his mother, sister to me, lies
dying in a city distant from this village
which once formed our core, unravelled
many years before. Look how we've hurt
us all so diligently, as time allowed.
You do look well, I say with some truth
and you need no stick to balance against
which bodes well for your decline. And I
am happy cocooned in my life and yes
it is sad we failed to keep in touch
but at least we have sunshine over mist.
