People get vicious when you fail
to appreciate their concern. They take
to looks and words; a push, a spit.
How dare I disrespect their sympathy?
They gift it from their heart, no
performance involved. I should be
consoled that they see me, hear me.

The sun is out today, the wind chills
through the window I opened for the cat.
I can stay in; there's plenty of food
safe in the fridge, and I have
a solid wall to stare at.
