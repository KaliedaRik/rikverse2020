That way a dog scrunches its snout
as eyes narrow, teeth show: incisor;
canine; heavy saliva drip? People
get right vicious when you – I – fail
to "appreciate" their concern. They take
to narrow-hot-eye, snarl-flare-nose. Wet
words: how dare I disrespect a sympathy?
They gift it from their heart, no
performance involved! I should be yes
consoled that they see me, hear me …

The sun is out today, the wind chills
through the window I opened for the cat.
I can stay in; there's plenty of food
safe in the fridge, and I have
a solid brick of wall to stare at.
