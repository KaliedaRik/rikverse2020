You loved your dad and he loved you.
He never visited our home, not once.
He is a translucence in my history: why
was that, mum? What kept him away from
our door, the comfort of a shared meal?
So many things I forgot to ask before
your recollections and fibs softened.

I remember many things from the few times
I met your dad. His coastal living room full
of the nautical, though he never served
on a ship. The quiet smell of his boiled
onions. Orchids and pristine Dolittle books,
not to be smirched by small fingers.

I think I saw him in a pub in our village,
one time, after he died. I think I was drunk.
No, it leaves me cold, the fact that this man
you loved so well was barely shared with me.
How can I open his covers now you're gone?
