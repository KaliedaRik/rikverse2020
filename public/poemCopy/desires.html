(After Επιθυμίες by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

Like beautiful, stilled bodies who failed to age
and laid to rest, with tears, inside a bright mausoleum,
with roses by their heads and jasmine at their feet –
such did these desires look like when they passed
without fulfilment: with not one of them granted
the pleasure of a night, or a soft-lit morning.
