(After Μακρυά by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

I'd like to relate this memory…
But it seems erased now… gone from recollection –
for it nests far away, in my young adolescence.

Skin layered from jasmine…
That August – was it August? – that evening…
I barely see the eyes now, they were, maybe, violet…
Ah, yes – violet, sapphirine violet.
