These words contain zero me;
I am an artefact of my flux brain.
I ate bread this morning, seared
between bright electric coils
and spread with churned, stabilised
oil that may have been pressed
from olives – though not virgin.
My toast existed until I ate it.
Thus my toast becomes me, thus I
am become these words, which hold
zero of me, see? Yea, the world
spindles its helix through space!
