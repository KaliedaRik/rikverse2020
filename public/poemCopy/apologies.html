I remember that time I came home from school
with nits in my hair and you had to pay
to fumigate the furniture – sorry, mum.

I remember that time when I was learning
to wank in the bath and things got intense
and a scream below and I came out half wrapped
in towel and bounded downstairs and there
I found you by the oven, dinner half-burned,
wearing a sodden kitchen ceiling – sorry, mum.

I remember that time I came home from school
and walked into the wrong house as we'd moved
again, you and I, and my bed for the night
was a floor in a corner with a mattress made
of chair cushions and I said: no. I walked away
and went home, to dad, and the bed you made me
abandon after he hit you, and how could I know
how I collapsed you, the fear I made you shoulder
when you, too, moved back – sorry, mum.
