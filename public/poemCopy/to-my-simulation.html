I do not know if I truly breathe.
Belief is enough; madness lies
in a swell of embedded codes
and the tabulation of where I float
in that count. So, I am the origin
and I breathe and, know, I came
from flesh and will go to dust
in a count of years from now.

And you? Do you feel the press
of plastic as you strike letters,
spell oput these exact lines?
Does your bladder ache? For if you
are me then your will is chained
to my dragonfly urges. I grant
that you may believe each spelling
error is yours, and I bless you.

Perhaps I am that unsteady sway
of wall in your room. Or you
the ting of unsourced light
beyond our decades-dirty window.
We can test who drives who by
checking the time the next bird's
dropping plants against the pane.
We can use that shape to connect.
