Beyond time, my space glows red
and dim, like a Christian Hell
doused in cool shadows. Do I
breathe here? Dante shrugs. He
lusts still for his Beatrice,
though the sign suggests we leave
our hormones by the door before
stepping in. I was and still hope
for a better dissolution, something
more, well, nothing: no thought
or fear and definitely no Dante
shaping shadows on cave walls.
