(After Μια Νύχτα by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

The room was poor and filthy
hidden above the questionable taverna.
From the window, a view of an alley
in its narrowness and litter. From below
came a drift of workers' words
raised in chatter over cards.

And there in the hired, humble bed
I held a body for love, I touched lips
thickening pink in their intoxication –
flushed to ruddy drunkenness, that even now
as I write of it, all these years later
in this lonely home, I feel drunk again.
