Within the nets, a bed, a history
set in layers beyond the reach of touch:
an archaeology of hints, so much
impression shaped in sense, this mystery
– it threatens me! What if this thread of thought
breaks as I brush it free from deep recall
of places, people, hurts and faults – so small
it sits, this hollow image only caught
in a nose-twitch: a flush of soap-washed wool
above, your skirt, its swirl of comfort pleats
so close for me to grab, yet it defeats
my grizzling fingers! "Stand up straight; don't pull!"
I tug, it snaps – the smell is gone again
and height surrounds me: only legs remain.
