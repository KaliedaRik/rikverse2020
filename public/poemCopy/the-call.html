Light a candle and watch it flame
in the clear mirror, thrice blown
clean-eyed on our night of the dead.
Remove all images of pumpkins, blood
from our minds: this is not that; this
is our space between space beside time.

Lift to our mouths the sound of the name
of the blessing we will, one who is known
beyond loss of breath. Let the sound spread
across the glass and bring it to flood
his caught time, bound space, in the mist
from which we emerge, to which we sublime.

I know they seek me. Am I to bear blame
for calamities? Must I reap what was sown?
Each loss is a stroke, they steep in my head
like dye on the cloth, like madder in mud.
We are apart, untogethered: as much as I miss
my heart's kin I douse their light, decline.
