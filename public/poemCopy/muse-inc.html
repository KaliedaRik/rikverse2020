Hello? Is that the strophe support hotline?
I need to report a problem – hold, what?
Yes, I am a registered user; yes, I paid
my verse charge last May. You need my what?
I was born a fat week after the equinox
as the trees let fall their ripened fruit
– is that okay? Good, now I'm having an issue
with my crafting – no! You do not dare
to transfer me! I've been transferred thrice
already, and if I'm forced to listen to
your on-hold muzak again there will be walls
covered in the gore of a thousand bulls
sacrificed on the altars of some stock plated
dividend and I promise they will be your walls!
Okay, my problem? Yes, every time I settle
to compose the lines tumble towards grief
and I'm beginning to think there's a glitch
in my subconscious modules – have I been what?
No, I can rhyme fine, though the titled oafs
that claim grace as high art gatekeepers seem
less keen on such work these days. And don't
start me on their demands for trauma porn!
Like I want to write poor little abused me
all day? So, how can I get out of this loop
… hello? Well I don't care if the readout
says I've agreed to conditioned terms. No, I
refuse to construct my best lyrics for a book
with the working title "Deathing By Numbers"
so for the love of Eternal Aten: make it stop!
