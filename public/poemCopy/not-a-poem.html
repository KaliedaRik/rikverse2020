Dear Mum. I have tried to write this poém
fifteen, maybe sixteen times. So easy
to lose count. I've debated with my head
if I should even care for the poém;
it might be better to let our stories
fester and wither, decline in my mind?
But givers who care insist the poém
could help me heal, perhaps. It's so easy
to type words; they tell nothing much. So, no:
no verse today. Your ash is not poém.
