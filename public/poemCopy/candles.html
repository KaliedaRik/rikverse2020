(After Κεριά by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

The days yet to pass stretch before us
like a column of candles in ranks –
golden and warm, delicate candles.

The days that are gone stay behind us,
a sad series of snuffed candles:
those closest still let drift smoke,
cold candles, melted and twisted.

I do not want to see; their forms sadden me,
it saddens me more to recall that first light.
I look ahead to the still lit candles.

I do not want to look back, lest I be scared
by how quickly the dark ranks lengthen,
how fast the dead candles multiply.
