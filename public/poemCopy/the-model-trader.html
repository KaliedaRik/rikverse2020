Today I have two heads.
Yesterday, three. Before:
more. I collect heads
and sometimes I sell them
as needs arise. Profit
in the trading of heads
is an art. So much depends
on the mapping of points
and the ability to swap out
eyes, and teeth. One head
I maintain for personal use
while the others I use
for show. Mostly. I try
not to sentimentalise heads
and it's good to keep
a wide selection ready
for whatever today brings.

I used to trade in hearts,
but they can be fragile;
they spoil when watched.
It needs a subtle spin
to keep a worthy heart
prime, and whole.
