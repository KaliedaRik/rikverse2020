If you want it, you earn it.
If you need it, fetch it yourself.
Stand with brothers; fight alone.
There's no free gift; there's no
leg up. We're born in shit, we dig
in sweat; we make what's ours, we take
what we can. Nobody cares, son, not
for us, for real. Salute for coin; kneel
for notes, as needs must. Earn your place
at the table and hold this family whole:
you're here for reasons. We love you. Now
stop that grizzling and eat them peas.
