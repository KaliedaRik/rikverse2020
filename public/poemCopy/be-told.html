If you want it, you earn it.
If you need it, go fetch it.
Stand with brothers; fight alone.
There's no free gift, no leg up.
We're born in shit, dig in sweat;
we make what's ours, take as can.
No one cares, son, not for us,
for real, just words and their wants.
Salute for coin; kneel for notes,
as needs must, to keep your place
at home: you're here for reasons.
You hold this family whole.
We love you. Now stop grizzling
and eat the rest of them peas.
