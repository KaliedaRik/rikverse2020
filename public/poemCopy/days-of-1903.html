(After Μέρες του 1903 by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

I don't see them anymore – they're lost already …
the poetic eyes, the wan
face … in the street's twilight …

I don't see them – picked up purely by chance,
I gave them up so easily
and then, for want, wanted them back.
The poetic eyes, the wan face,
I don't see those lips anymore.
