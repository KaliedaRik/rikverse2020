This September and midway done with it.
There should be leaves on the ground:
chestnuts feel loose without a mulch
to blanket that ripe, red wood. A vain
wish for order. I learned I could take
husks and boil them for dye but dying
is not my concern this turn. I'm done
with it, see. Some harvests cut deeper
than a pile of leaves you taught me
to kick through, laugh loud and alive
to change and good with the growth of it.

I cannot wrap the conker back into its crib.
I've lost the lesson for fixing things.
