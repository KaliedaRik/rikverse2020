This morning I learn about kairos: the measure
of moments, the taste of fate, my rash decision
to feel my breaths, not count them. Numeration
bothers me; no, meetings bother me, I found pleasure

this morning in my shifty failure to click on a link
to take my pixel face to that space where colleagues
gathered to count work done, planned, stalled. Plagues
can stalk fallen rooms, homes, loves and still we think

a well attended spreadsheet can help mend the day.
I want to slot kairos into my ticket: a time to plant,
a time to pluck up that which is sown. I would repent
my collecting of deaths but, sorry, I cannot find a way

to find space in our cloudy, pastel calendar. I did open
a spike for the issue, find a fix, perhaps soon, even.
