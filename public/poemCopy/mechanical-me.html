I am no golem. The words
that soak my head offer
no guidance. A flower
is a form of petals
and purpose: be more.

A golem, hear, is a roast
of broken pots, set
anew with slip, words
fired in the glaze:
instructions to obey
and follow; productive.

I am no flower. Words
describe the petals
yet fail to pulse
saps to set seeds.
My seed? I spilt it,
deliberate, I lack
the will to breed.

A small, tight clockwork
of springs and wheels
can settle good words
to the press, ink meaning
to minds that would admire
a furl of petal if only
they had the quiet know
of my ticking existence.
