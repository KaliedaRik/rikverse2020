To dream, to slip from ache; to taste silence
while sated in peace. To watch the sheens
unfold in weaves and pleats; to travel, meet,
recover, love, anneal with family and friends.

I need a foot to hop, a leg; flesh. This is
an inconvenience, I tell my greeting guides.
The All Book you offer to let me subsume lies
yonder from this dream and I lack the recipe
for whipping bone, muscle, skin from aether.
That, I feel, is in the Book – page millions
and some, your gift of electrum inked know.

I drift in serenity, unlocked. Hosts surrounding
my untidy radiance manifest with twain-taut
auras: sensual; touched, even: I – I come?
