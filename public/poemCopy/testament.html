I have attended too many funerals this year
and I have views. When did we start avoiding
the whole corpse? I am more than a brief quip.

I have caused joy, at times, sometimes planned,
though my ability to conjure mirth is as calculated
as surf across pebbles. I am more than salt washes.

I have stoked anger in the chests and raw throats
and fists of people I love beyond bow-wrapped gifts.
Think of me as you step by my stone; rage as you piss.

I have discovered the soft sanctuary of withdrawal
and I have sorrow. Sometimes the overwhelm became
more than an imagined rock best set beyond my hearth.

I have, perhaps, spread wisdom in words I learned
to cultivate towards some telic lyric, yet each phrase
leavened as I listened, hard, to words beyond my skull.

I have found love beyond the physics of friction,
the chemical pulses that current my veins, and love
found me in that smile that folds your face in trust.

I have attended so many funerals and one day I'll come
to a celebration by a different route, and when that
impossible punchline lands, know this: know all of me

as you gather, wear a full pageant of feelings, recall
me entire, unedited, rough as a mud scrub and ready
to settle where tides push me, dissolute peace.
