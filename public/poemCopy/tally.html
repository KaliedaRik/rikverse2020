While the online tests
classify me easily
as a drained Introvert
or Architect archetype
I still counted the reactions
I received to the posts I made
for my Mother's death.

I am not as disconnected
as I think I am.
Some of my best ideas arrive
while you talk at me.

I need to frame this poem.
Massage your opinion of me
through warnings ahead of triggers.
I like triggers; I enjoy tabulating
reactions to statements:
I have a t-shirt that claims
I am not a nice person.

My sister died last week and I
cannot summarise her.
It's complicated;
She was complicated.
My mother died last week and while
I eulogised her with good words
they are not the story.
Nothing is settled in words;
treaties are there to be broken.

"There's only room enough for one woman
in the kitchen," my sister told me,
angry. She married at nineteen.
A man with a stove is an escape.

"Let's have another baby, to save
our marriage," my mother told me,
angry. Hateful. It was not me she saw
as she remembered, I tell myself.
My mother loved me.

While the online tests
classify me easily
as a pitiful Introvert
or sinister Sigma Architect
plotting on sidelines
I shall still count reactions
to this poem. Your breakdown,
dear words, inspire me.
