"If William Shakespeare was alive today,
he would be on Instagram"
(Harman Kaur, 2018)

I never witnessed the moment
when you learned I was gay.
I wasn't there to share my news.

My sister told you, by phone.
I expect she had her reasons.

So there we were, you in shock,
me with a leaflet in my hand:
how to cope with a queer child.

You taught me a long time before
about the power of secrets:
you and dad knew how to set
my fuse, when best to light it.

My sister never witnessed
the moment I told you she was
pregnant. I told you by phone.

I was at work that day.
She asked me to tell you.
I didn't ask her why.

I never told my sister
about my rainbow virus.
I didn't want to worry you.

Some secrets are best kept
in reserve, just in case.

Isn't that true, mum?
