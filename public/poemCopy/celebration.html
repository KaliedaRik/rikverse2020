Perception and reality dance around the coffin.
Per wears soft slippers and contoured tights;
Rea bears the ribbed remains of elastic stockings.
Their glide is slow, hands locked in wide angles.
The coffin burned yesterday: a private disposal.

On a large screen, a slideshow. Grained childhoods
mixed with blurred poses and modern pixel perfection:
unplanned moments; unpracticed half-smiles. Faults.
In a corner of this hired room a travelling disco
thumps old beats: "I met my love by the gas works wall".

I know four people in this crowd: that is the metric
of my separation, that and twenty years of planned calls
undialled, visits to meet postponed. You made a good life
in this unwelcome city, sis. It breaks me when your laugh
spears me from posed sibling snaps: so much gossip I have!
