I do not want to live forever. Days
can drag when not constrained by night, then when
the light departs, I start. I hunt alone
in twilight hours; I find new mysteries
to worry at - the who of you; the why
of us together, us apart. We live
in moments snatched from time and sometimes I
lose sight of what I am. The world explodes
around me shiny bright and clang and stench
and altogether smashed to shapes that make
no sense, no need, no story stabbed in clay
and even Gilgamesh must die, his words
a grit of brittle tablets cracked by rocks
that flow and catch and break to tremors, smacks
to change the shape of empires, nations gone
and what of me, of us? I do not want
to live forever if it means I lose
my memories of you: I want to live
with you, for you, tomorrow, touch by hug
and kiss by tear and drag by our snatched tick.
