I see it written on cheap shopping bags,
envelopes, the mirror I sometimes check:
don't look back. Bold words pop up in cooking
details on my prepared meals, on the box
I type my password in. Somebody knifed them
into the table I sit at: don't look back.

What instead, I ask? What if I need to twist
my neck? You instruct me to forfeit a glance,
dear phrase, yet offer no reasons, no rhyme
beyond a suspect whisper: it's better this way.

Look forward. Harvest happy images, yes, but
keep them in the corner where they can gather
old skin and lost mites. Polish them with love
and move away, move on. And don't look back!
