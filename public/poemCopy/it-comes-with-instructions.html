I see it written on the shopping bags,
envelopes, the mirror I sometimes check:
don't look back. The words pop up in cooking
details on my prepared meals, on the box
I type my password in. Somebody scraped them
into the table I sit at: don't look back.

What instead, I ask? I need the tang of salt
in my life; you ask me to forfeit gifts
and yet where's the payoff? Sometimes I need
blood thrust through tensed vein to move me.

Look forward. Gather happy images, yes, but
keep them in the corner where they can gather
old skin and lost mites. Polish them with love
and move on, move on. Just don't look back!
