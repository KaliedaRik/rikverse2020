Why am I thinking of cheeks? Eyes open or closed,
no difference: I see cheeks. Your cheek, mainly.
I see it flat from aside your ear, me caught in a lean
and your skin its own perfume in my sinus. I see
that faint blush of soft hair, as if powdered but no,
only bleached by age. I know I am face blind, a gift
from you, and my brain seeks specifics where others
find the whole without thinking it through – such
a magical trick! So yes, it was going to be a part
of your face that triggers me … but your cheek?
Take the stupid tears as I stoop behind you, caught,
to kiss your memory "see you later" and, yeah, laugh!
