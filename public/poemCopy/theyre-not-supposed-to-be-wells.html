I don't like the way eyes sink
into faces, holes drilled and filled
with not-skin. The brows and nose
look like the edge walls of a boomed
volcano, a face that met air beyond
the womb and exploded so, top-blown,
the person inside could grow into
their caldera of steam-pop desires. Hair
worries me: if it's good enough for brows
why not swirl into sockets? I could stroke
a fur-lined burrow with my finger
until I touch the person trapped
in their skull. Eyes are wrong, mate,
so wrong and mine keep flooding and
I've lost the bucket for bailing
salt and fuck you for seeing me hurt!
