So now let sit, let out and close my eyes
and stop the thoughts. No words, no whispers here,
be here behind the breath, each breath a sear
of chilling air I catch and pack inside
my chest. Don't think, explore, explain: exhale!
My shoulders ache from weights I strain to see;
my back, my hips, they shake inside – no! Be
the lack of thought. Be still! Learn to be frail.
This trial of loss is not a lesson, not
a puzzle. There is nothing here to solve,
no patterns, plans to dodge or even know:
just sit and breathe and let the weights unknot
and sheet to lead to line my graves. Absolve
my tight-clipped fright and let me grieve, let go …
