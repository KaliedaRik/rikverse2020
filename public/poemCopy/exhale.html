And on the last day –
peace; saplings reach for white clouds
mushrooming the sky.

Still is the air at the foot
of the stairs: too still. Your ribs
move so fast to fight life into lungs.
No kick in your legs. Your eyes wide,
your tongue long; weightless air
and I ache to assure you but words
won't move through this glue of gas
wrapping your ribs on the wrong side.

In the clinic the medic nods, agrees
no more can be done. I watch a nurse
needle your skin, a final slash
seeking a vein. I know it must be done:
so long we've waited for this day
to come, tasting the loss long before
dawn found you at the foot of the stairs,
broken, beyond refurbishment.

Fuck you for ripping my guts to shreds!
Fuck you for suspending my howls in brine!

And on that last morning –
an ease: a slow push of poisons to stop
your ribs and your heart and your hurt
and outside I see saplings bend
in the late spring breeze
and mushrooming clouds
boil through blue skies.
