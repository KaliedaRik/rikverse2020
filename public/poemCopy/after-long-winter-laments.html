That moment between the brown of spent daffodils
and the damp redolence of first-trimmed grass.

That place where each day the magpie nest masses
in sticks and bicycle spokes and stolen kisses.

That purpose, to fracture like buds on the coppiced
copper beech and unfurl to a fuzz of soft greens.

That movement you tumble in me to take you
from lost to discovered and map us in inks.

That melody: soon, my hidden heartache, soon
we shall widen our beaks in dawn song.
