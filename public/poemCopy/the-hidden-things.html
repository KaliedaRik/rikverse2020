(After Κρυμμένα by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

From all that I've done, from what I have said,
let them not parse that effort to find me.
An obstacle held there; it transformed
my actions, my habits of life.
An obstacle stood there, stopped me,
caught words when I would speak.
My most unnoticed dealings
and my most hidden writing –
only from there will they feel me.
But perhaps it is not worth it, to pay
so much care, such effort, to analyse me.
And after – in that most perfect community –
some other me, made as I'm made, will surely
appear and push beyond the limits, and freely.
