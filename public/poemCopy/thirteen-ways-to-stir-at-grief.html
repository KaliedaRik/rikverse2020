# Deny
If I break the wings
of the words I hear
they don't arrive?

# Investigate
Count the days
between birthdays: they
add up, they
break hips; they
overwhelm.

# Lust
Rabbits at rut by the Lea.
This is not a game.
I ache.

# Anger
There's still water in the river:
it still flows; it ripples. I see
its bound curve. I feel
its wet slaps.

# Distract
If there were sheep on the Marshes
at Walthamstow, I would count
against sheep. I count
horses, cattle, steps. I count
faces on strangers. I count
stirs of words, snatched through air.
I will count.

# Perform
These boots are suitably black:
they balance me.
They can step with me
in them.

# Bargain
I have many memories
of you.
Burnt simnel. See?
I say this recall stirs me better
than our last touch.

# Numb
Numb.
Number.
Numbering.
Let me count the ways.

# Refuse
This work, the work people
pay me to perform. I will
not do it! Watch me instead
type: rabbit.

# Depress
I at too small for hugs.
"It is a significant trauma that you endure."
"We offer many services for the recently bereaved."
"There is no shame in reaching out for help."
I at too small for hugs.

# Frustrate
I can stir words as I stare, score.
"Rabbits"
one, and no, two
at rut? Perhaps one.

# Accept
I keep a small part of my mother
in a vial. She glows
at night. I sit her in the corner
with my boxes:
a treasure of cats.

# Reminisce
I still wear the cool imprint
of your hand on my cheek.
That last memory stirs our first
recall and it
is good.
Perhaps.
