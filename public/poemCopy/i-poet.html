They drained a sea for cotton, that whitest of riches.
Fishing boats rust in the cuddle of salt-crust dunes.
The video I watch tells of catastrophe, swollen lungs
over empty bellies, harsh cold when the sun falls south.
Admire this fine calf I have tied to the Asherah pole.

They drained a sea for sheep, that fattest of riches.
Grass grows across fenced clay chalks amid the tight coils
of ditches and sluices; the fleece and meat is hale, good.
My land, this strand of my birth. My history and wealth.
Admire this fine calf I have tied to the Asherah pole.

They drained a sea for dreams, those fleetest of riches.
A world of totems and archetypes come to dance as the sun
falls away to burn over impossible lands and lost seas.
I harvest dead relatives, displaced love for words, fame.
Admire this fine calf I have tied to the Asherah pole.
