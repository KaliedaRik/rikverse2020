I took a chisel and hammer to the walls
of this house and that house, your house
even and built walls from the baked blocks
I stole: here a care for tidy work, there
a disregard for gossip. Some bricks matched
a foundation I dug from mud, an outline,
and others I gathered for their rough-tough
textures veined over faces. For mortar I chose
gutter mud and moss bedding. This chimney?
I like how it spires, though I lack the know
of grates and flues. We can illustrate smoke
using dry ice. Someone told me that painting
a home on bolted plywood is an easy construct
to clean but a lazy mind is a dull place
for a safe nest and while cemeteries are nice
for a visit I couldn't settle down in one.
