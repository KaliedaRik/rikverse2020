The gift of a baby can be a fraught concept:
it tends to arrive with a surprising range
of costs beyond nappies.

(God can be kind to his cherubs, I've heard, sometimes.)

Have you considered a mynah, instead?
They sit in a cage all day, and can learn
to say: 'love you!'

A car can lead to smiles and joy-tears, though
she will need lessons to take advantage
of her gifted freedom.

(Is this dreamline even open? Can he hear me whisper?)

A gift of fresh life needs a negotiation
for the best outcome, so you will lose that edge
of surprise, dad.

I mean, we don't live in your old man's youth
anymore, when a demanded tryst in the marriage bed
was just a delight.

(I know your mother said it worked for her, in the end.)

I hear chocolates can save a marriage.
I also hear not sleeping with other men's wives
can help a bit.

And I know you can love! There's a fierceness that burns
in your current brood and that is your gift to them.
Can they not fix you?

(God can be kind to the least of his cherubs, sometimes.)

I mean, it's really your decision. And hopefully
hers, too. But all I can bring to the bargain
are my screams, and shit.
