Today I shall not be
Jesus. I shall not judge
or raise the dead. Today
I can admire God's hands
amid the bronzed sycamore
leaves, let the susser
of their rustle nurse
me to see the still
of us. No tick or date
or deadline. Today I want
to watch your living breath
smile, and I am with you
and my hand curves
across your shoulder
and down to your waist
and we kiss, right here
together in the pause:
a hammer over a nail
over the coffin's
heartwood lid.
