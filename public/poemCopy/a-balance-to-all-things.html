I rearranged the bottles
in my wine rack, for
symmetrical reasons.
I have a wine rack;
I don't drink.

I would like a gym membership
though I don't much like gyms,
not nowadays, the way
the machines align
to fit some grid.

I prefer to walk to see changes,
woodland sounds, my slow effort
to step me towards a calm.
I like calm, I think;
I rarely walk, now.

I rearranged my life to heal
the hole that your dying
knitted into it. You showed
me how to cast on. I like
wool; I struggle to
cast you off.

See what you made me do?
Our lyric lacks balance:
I itch to fix it.
