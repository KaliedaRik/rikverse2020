Dying is easy: surviving is hard.
It's an old rub, and yet still true.
Which irks my teeth: the easy part
comes last. Dying takes sweat. Who

denies they spend thought on that end?
Their end? At lunch? Caught in traffic?
In the edged eyes of loved ones, friends
taken for strangers in a moment's panic?

I have no interest in war: war cares
deeply for me - another eeled aphorism
whose quick slip scratches my gut. I despair
at my insights: they fret. We carry prisons

atop spines and call them rich culture.
Ending is easy in my season of strife
yet a foot's lift can startle my future.
Mechanical, that step. Breathe in for life.

Breathe in for more norms. Breathe hard.
Make each breath a sprint, but where
to run, friend? Dying is easy! My heart
hangs a beat as loved eyes fall cold, as air

settles in nostrils as around me the war
roars on. Keats at least took his leaves
as truth, though they soaked in the hue
of his clot lungs. What mellow lies I weave.
