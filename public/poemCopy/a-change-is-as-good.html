5 July 2024:
"It's been a long night's entertainment, here in London"

This moment, that mark. The time for buds
and eggs and hopes is done. We witnessed
the ruby rose – so long a wish – pluck
its thorns, unfurl and wilt, and bloom.
We glimpsed an egg hatch an orange chick,
soon fledged, aloft in tricks and capers
to catch capricious cameras. A nation's tree
in Hadrian's Gap, felled by vandals, stretched
new twigs, soft stems to twist and fight
a roiled, turquoise sky. Green sepals, leaves;
green eggshell scattered like hen's teeth.

But what will change, today? I've watched
so many parades wend this way. I've tallied
rainbows, cried as they leached from sight
in harsh, fresh false dawns. I know my eyes
are dry and gray: yet to feel the dessications
spread so wide – it stabs my throat, my heart!
