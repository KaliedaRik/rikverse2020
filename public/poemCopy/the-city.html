(After Η Πόλις by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

You said: "I will go to another land, another sea.
Another city will be found, better than this one.
All of my efforts, each of them writes a doom;
and this heart of mine – as if it's dead – buried.
How long will my mind stay in this wasting state?
Whatever I turn my eyes to face, wherever I look
I see the charred remains of my life here:
so many years I spent ruining, destroying it."

You'll never find another land, another sea.
The city, it follows you. You'll return to its streets
unchanged. It's in these quarters you'll grow old
and in these solid houses that you'll turn white.
You'll always come to this city. For beyond – don't hope –
there's no ship for you, there's no road.
Just as you've worked to ruin your life here
in this home, its wreckage spreads the world over.
