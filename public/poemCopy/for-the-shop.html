(After Του Mαγαζιού by Κωνσταντίνος Καβάφης)

He wraps them with neatness and care
in some green and precious silk.

From rubies, a rose; from pearls, a lily.
From amethysts, violets. As he judges,

he wills it: his eye crafts them in beauty, not nature
as seen or studied. He'll keep them in the safe,

examples of his bold and capable work.
In the shop, as a buyer enters, he picks

others from cases, sells them – famous finery:
bracelets, chains, necklaces, rings.
